# Animals

This policy covers pets. Assistance dogs are not pets and nothing here applies
to them; see the accessibility policy.

## Where pets can travel

Pets travel on the Kilmore route only. The Estuary vessels and MV Ardmore carry
no pets, so there is no way to take a pet to Rensley Point or on the Harbour
Circular.

The limit is six animals per sailing, counted at booking. A seventh booking is
refused rather than waitlisted, because the pet area cannot be extended and
standby would mean an animal turned away at the terminal.

## Fare and booking

£4.00 per animal on the Kilmore route, booked in advance with the passenger
booking. Pets cannot be added at the terminal, because the six-animal limit is
checked at booking and terminal staff cannot see the count for the sailing.

## On board

Dogs must be on a lead and under control at all times on deck. Cats, rabbits and
other small animals must be in a closed carrier.

Animals are not permitted in the passenger lounge, the cafe or the bridge deck.
The pet area is on the upper deck aft, which is covered but not heated, and has
water bowls and four kennels. Kennels are first come, first served on the day
and cannot be reserved.

An animal may be left in a vehicle on the vehicle deck during the crossing.
Passengers cannot return to the vehicle deck once the crossing is under way, so
an animal left in a vehicle is unattended for the full 55 minutes, and the
company will not carry an animal left in a vehicle in ambient temperatures above
22 degrees.

## Animals we do not carry

Livestock, horses and any animal requiring a specialist transport arrangement
are not carried on passenger sailings. Reptiles, birds and small mammals are
carried in closed carriers under the ordinary pet fare and count against the
six-animal limit.

A dog subject to a control order or of a type restricted by law is carried only
with muzzle and lead, and the booking must state it. A dog that arrives muzzled
where the booking did not say so is carried, and the discrepancy is logged.

## Behaviour

Crew may require an animal to be muzzled, moved to a kennel or returned to a
vehicle if it is distressed or is distressing other passengers. This is the
crew's decision and is not a matter for the complaints process, though the
decision itself is recorded.

Fouling on deck is the owner's responsibility to report to a crew member
immediately. Cleaning materials are held at the information desk.
