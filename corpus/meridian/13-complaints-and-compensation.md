# Complaints and compensation

## How a complaint is handled

A complaint may be made at a terminal, by email, or by post. Every complaint is
acknowledged within 3 working days and answered substantively within 20 working
days. Where an investigation will take longer, the complainant is told why and
given a date, which is not itself an extension of the 20-day target: the target
is recorded as breached.

A complaint about a crew member's safety decision, including a decision not to
sail or to require an animal to be muzzled, is answered with an explanation
rather than a review of the decision. Those decisions are not overturned by the
complaints process.

## Compensation authority

| Role | May authorise up to |
|---|---|
| Duty Manager | £50 |
| Operations Manager | £250 |
| Director | No limit |

These limits are for discretionary compensation. The entitlements under the
disruption policy, meaning refunds, transfers, meal allowances and overnight
accommodation, are not compensation and are not subject to these limits. Staff
do not need authority to give a passenger what they are already owed, and a
passenger should never have to complain to receive it.

## What we do not compensate for

Consequential losses are not compensated: a missed connection, a missed
appointment, a booked night's accommodation at the destination, or lost earnings.
This is stated at the point of sale. Passengers with a connection to make are
advised to travel on an earlier sailing than the connection strictly requires,
and this advice does not create a liability when it is followed and the earlier
sailing is also cancelled.

## Escalation

A complainant who is not satisfied may ask for a review by a Director, which is
answered within a further 20 working days. Beyond that, the complaint may be
taken to the passenger transport ombudsman, whose details are given in every
final response, including responses the complainant is likely to accept.

## 2024 in numbers

| Measure | 2024 |
|---|---|
| Complaints received | 1,340 |
| Acknowledged within 3 working days | 97% |
| Answered within 20 working days | 82% |
| Median time to substantive answer | 14 working days |
| Escalated to Director review | 84 |
| Referred to the ombudsman | 11 |
| Discretionary compensation paid | £41,200 |

Sixty-one per cent of complaints concerned the winter timetable: sailings
cancelled at short notice, and the five-sailing winter service itself. Both
trace to the withdrawal of MV Lachlan in March 2024 and the decision not to fund
a replacement. The complaints process cannot fix either, and answering 61% of
complaints with an explanation the complainant already knows is the main reason
the median response time rose from 9 working days in 2023.

The 18% of complaints answered outside 20 working days are concentrated in
January and February, the months with both the highest complaint volume and the
lowest office staffing.
