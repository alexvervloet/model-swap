# Lost property

## Where items go

Items found on board are handed to a crew member and logged into the vessel's
lost property book before the end of the sailing day. Items found in a terminal
go to the terminal office. Everything is transferred to the Port Alben lost
property store within 48 hours, so a passenger who lost something at Rensley
Point on Monday should ask at Port Alben from Wednesday.

## How long items are held

| Category | Held for |
|---|---|
| General items | 90 days |
| Items of value above £50 | 6 months |
| Identity documents, bank cards | 30 days, then destroyed securely |
| Medication | 7 days |
| Perishable food and drink | Disposed the same day |
| Prams, wheelchairs, mobility aids | 6 months, and actively traced |

Mobility aids are traced rather than shelved: the terminal office contacts
bookings from the sailing on which the item was found, because somebody is
without it.

## Claiming

Claims are made in person at the Port Alben terminal office or by email. A
claimant describes the item before being shown it, and identification is
required for anything above £50 in value and for any identity document.

An item can be posted at the claimant's cost, except identity documents and bank
cards, which are never posted and must be collected or destroyed.

## Unclaimed items

At the end of the holding period, unclaimed items are donated to a local charity
or disposed of. Meridian does not sell unclaimed property and does not permit
staff to take it. Items of value above £50 are logged individually on disposal
with the date and the destination.

## What we do not hold

Cash found loose is logged and held for 90 days, then passed to charity in full.
Meridian does not hold cash beyond that period and does not pay a finder.

Bicycles left in the racks at either terminal are not lost property. They are
removed after 14 days, logged, and held a further 30 days before disposal.
