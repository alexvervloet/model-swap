# Cancellations and refunds

This policy covers cancellations made by the passenger. When Meridian cancels a
sailing, the disruption policy applies instead and overrides everything here,
including the treatment of non-refundable fares.

## Passenger cancellations

Refunds depend on the fare class and, for Standard fares, on how much notice is
given before the scheduled departure time.

| Notice before departure | Flex | Standard | Saver |
|---|---|---|---|
| More than 48 hours | Full refund | Full refund | None |
| 12 to 48 hours | Full refund | 50% refund | None |
| Less than 12 hours | Full refund | None | None |
| After departure | Full refund not available | None | None |

Notice is measured from the time the cancellation is received by the company,
not the time the passenger decided to cancel. A cancellation submitted through
the website is timestamped on receipt.

## Amendments

A Standard ticket may be moved to another sailing for a £3.00 amendment fee plus
any difference in fare, provided the request is made before the original
departure. The fee is per ticket, not per booking. Flex tickets amend free.
Saver tickets cannot be amended, and a passenger who wants a different sailing
must buy a new ticket.

## Missed sailings

A passenger who arrives after the gate has closed has missed the sailing. See
the terminal operations policy for gate closing times. A missed sailing is
treated as a cancellation with no notice: Standard and Saver tickets have no
value, and a Flex ticket may be used on a later sailing that day.

## Vehicle bookings

A vehicle booking cancelled with less than 12 hours notice forfeits the vehicle
fare regardless of fare class, including Flex. Deck space held and unused cannot
be resold at that notice, and this is the one place where a Flex ticket does not
refund in full. The foot passenger fares on the same booking follow the table
above as normal.

## How refunds are paid

Refunds are made to the original payment method within 10 working days of
approval. Where the original method has expired or been closed, the refund is
made by bank transfer on production of account details, and the 10 working days
run from receipt of those details rather than from the cancellation.

Refunds are never made in cash at a terminal, and terminal staff cannot approve
one.

## Group bookings

Bookings of 10 or more passengers may cancel up to 25% of the party with more
than 7 days notice at no charge. Beyond 25%, or inside 7 days, the cancelled
places follow the fare class table. The whole group booking cancelled inside 7
days forfeits the deposit, which is 20% of the total fare.
