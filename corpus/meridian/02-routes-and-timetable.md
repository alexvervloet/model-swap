# Routes and timetable

Meridian Ferries operates three routes. Sailing times below are scheduled
departure times; the crossing durations are typical and not guaranteed.

## Kilmore route

Port Alben to Kilmore, 55 minutes. Vehicles and foot passengers. Operated by the
Coastal vessels only.

**Summer timetable, 1 April to 31 October.** Eight sailings each way daily.
From Port Alben: 06:15, 08:00, 10:15, 12:30, 14:45, 17:00, 19:15, 21:30. From
Kilmore, each sailing departs 70 minutes after arrival.

**Winter timetable, 1 November to 31 March.** Five sailings each way daily.
From Port Alben: 07:40, 10:30, 13:15, 16:00, 18:45.

The winter reduction is not seasonal demand management. It is what two vessels
can sustain while the third covers refits and relief across the whole fleet,
and it dates from the withdrawal of MV Lachlan in March 2024.

Kilmore has a single berth. Two vessels cannot be alongside at Kilmore at the
same time, and the whole timetable is built around that constraint. A delay of
more than 40 minutes on one sailing therefore pushes every later sailing that
day rather than being absorbed.

## Ashcombe crossing

Ashcombe to Rensley Point, 18 minutes. Foot passengers and bicycles only. No
vehicles. Operated by the Estuary vessels.

**Summer.** Every 30 minutes, 06:00 to 22:00.
**Winter.** Hourly, 07:00 to 19:00.

No booking is taken on this route. Passengers travel on the next available
sailing, and a sailing at capacity means waiting for the following one.

## Harbour Circular

A 45-minute sightseeing loop from Port Alben, returning to Port Alben. Operated
by MV Ardmore.

**Season.** 1 April to 31 October only. Three departures daily: 11:00, 13:30,
16:00. There is no winter service on this route, and no substitute vessel is
provided when Ardmore is unavailable.

## Timetable changes

Seasonal timetables are published eight weeks before they take effect. Changes
inside that window are made only for vessel availability, crew shortfall or
harbour works, and are notified by email to every booking affected on the
sailings involved.
