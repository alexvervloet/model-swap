# Vehicles and bicycles

## Where vehicles can travel

Vehicles are carried on the Kilmore route only, on the Coastal vessels. The
Estuary vessels and MV Ardmore carry no vehicles of any kind, and there is no
route on which a vehicle can reach Rensley Point by ferry.

## Booking and standby

Vehicle space must be booked in advance. Twenty per cent of vehicle deck space
on each sailing is held back for standby and released at the terminal on the day
of travel, in arrival order. Standby is not a queue that carries over: a vehicle
not carried on one sailing rejoins standby for the next in the order it arrives,
not in the order it first queued.

## Size limits

| Limit | Standard | With prior approval |
|---|---|---|
| Length | 12 metres | 16 metres |
| Height | 4.2 metres | 4.5 metres |
| Width | 2.6 metres | 3.0 metres |

Anything above the standard limit requires seven days notice and written
approval from the Operations Manager, who will confirm which sailings can take
it. Approval is specific to a sailing, because it depends on what else is booked
onto the deck.

A vehicle that arrives larger than declared is refused carriage and the fare is
not refunded, because the space it needed was held for something else.

## Dangerous goods

Passengers must declare any of the following at check-in:

- LPG or camping gas cylinders. Maximum two cylinders per vehicle, none above
  15kg, valves closed and secured upright.
- Petrol in a container. Maximum one 10-litre container, sealed, in addition to
  the vehicle's own tank.
- Oxygen or medical gas. Permitted, must be declared, no quantity limit for
  personal medical use.

Anything not on this list that is classified as dangerous goods must be arranged
as freight and cannot travel on a passenger booking.

## Electric vehicles

Electric and hybrid vehicles travel normally and at the standard fare. Charging
is not available on board. Vehicles must be left with charging cables stowed
inside the vehicle, not run to any deck socket.

## Bicycles

Bicycle capacity is set by the racks fitted to each vessel; see the fleet
register for the numbers by class. Bicycles are secured by the passenger in the
racks provided and must not be left unsecured on deck.

**Kilmore route.** £2.00 per bicycle. Booking is not required but is strongly
recommended between June and August, when the racks fill on most sailings.

**Ashcombe crossing.** Free, no booking. The Estuary vessels carry twenty
bicycles each because the crossing is part of the coastal cycle route.

Tandems and cargo bicycles count as two spaces and are carried at two fares
where a fare applies. Trailers attached to a bicycle count as a further space.

## Batteries

Electric bicycles are carried normally with the battery fitted. A loose lithium
battery above 300Wh may not be carried in the passenger lounge and must be
stowed in the battery locker on the vehicle deck, which means loose batteries
above that size cannot travel on the Ashcombe crossing at all, as the Estuary
vessels have no vehicle deck and no locker.
