# Terminal operations

## Check-in and gate closing

| Route | Check-in opens | Check-in closes | Gate closes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Kilmore, vehicles | 60 minutes before | 30 minutes before | 10 minutes before |
| Kilmore, foot passengers | 60 minutes before | 15 minutes before | 5 minutes before |
| Ashcombe | Not required | Not required | 2 minutes before |
| Harbour Circular | 30 minutes before | 10 minutes before | 5 minutes before |

Gate closing times are absolute. A passenger arriving after the gate has closed
has missed the sailing, and the refund policy treats that as a cancellation with
no notice. Crew do not hold a sailing for a passenger who is in the terminal
building, because the sailing time is set by the tide and the berth at Kilmore
rather than by convenience.

A vehicle that has checked in but is not at the marshalling lane when loading
begins loses its space to standby.

## Boarding order

1. Passengers who have booked assistance, and any passenger who asks for extra
   time.
2. Foot passengers.
3. Vehicles, in marshalling lane order.

Disembarkation is the reverse, with vehicles first, then foot passengers, then
assisted passengers, so that nobody who needs time is moving among vehicles.

## Berths

Port Alben has three berths and can turn two vessels at once with the third
berth held for the Harbour Circular in season. Kilmore has one berth, which is
the binding constraint on the whole Kilmore timetable: a vessel cannot arrive
until the previous one has departed, and a delay above 40 minutes therefore
propagates to every later sailing that day rather than being absorbed.

Ashcombe and Rensley Point each have one berth and a fixed 12-minute turnaround.

## Overbooking

Sailings are not overbooked. Where a smaller vessel is substituted and the
booked load exceeds its capacity, the terminal office contacts the most recent
bookings first and offers a transfer to another sailing with the disruption
entitlements, including where the ticket is a Saver.

## Terminal staff authority

Terminal staff may:

- authorise the meal allowance and overnight accommodation under the disruption
  policy,
- release a held wheelchair space 20 minutes before departure,
- refuse carriage of a vehicle that exceeds its declared size,
- move a passenger to another sailing without a fee where the company has caused
  the change.

Terminal staff may not authorise a cash refund, override a fare class, or add a
pet to a sailing.

## Unattended items

An unattended bag in a terminal building is reported to the Duty Manager and, if
not claimed within 15 minutes, treated as a security incident under the incident
reporting policy. Items found on board or in a terminal and handed in go to lost
property.
