# Fares and tickets

All fares are single-journey unless stated. Return fares are twice the single
fare; there is no return discount.

## Fare classes

Every ticket is sold in one of three classes, and the class governs what can be
changed or refunded.

| Class | Change | Refund | Sold on |
|---|---|---|---|
| Flex | Free, any time before departure | Full, any time before departure | All routes |
| Standard | £3.00 amendment fee | By notice period, see the refunds policy | All routes |
| Saver | Not permitted | None | Kilmore route only, limited allocation |

Saver fares are released 60 days before departure in a fixed allocation per
sailing and are not reinstated once sold. A Saver ticket that goes unused has no
residual value.

| Child under 5 | Free |
| Bicycle | Free |
| Ten-trip book | £30.00 |

Ten-trip books are valid for twelve months from first use and are transferable
between passengers, but only one trip may be taken from a book per sailing.

## Harbour Circular

Adult £14.00, child 5 to 15 £7.00, under 5 free. Sold as a single class with
Flex conditions, because the sailing is either running or it is not.

## Kilmore resident discount

Residents of Kilmore registered with the company travel at 40% off the adult and
child foot passenger fares and 25% off vehicle fares on the Kilmore route.
Registration is annual, requires proof of address, and the discount is not
available on Saver fares, which are already discounted.

## Payment and validity

Tickets are valid only for the sailing named on them, with the exception of Flex
tickets, which may be used on any sailing on the same route on the same calendar
day without amendment. A ticket is not transferable to another person except a
ten-trip book.
