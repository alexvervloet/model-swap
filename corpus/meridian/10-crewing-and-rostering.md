# Crewing and rostering

## Minimum safe crewing

A vessel does not sail below these numbers. They are set by the safety
certificate for each vessel class and are not a target or a guideline.

| Class | Minimum crew | Composition |
|---|---|---|
| Coastal | 7 | Master, Mate, 2 Engineers, 3 Deck and Hospitality |
| Estuary | 3 | Master, Engineer, 1 Deck |
| Heritage | 4 | Master, Engineer, 2 Deck |

MV Ardmore carries four rather than three because the heritage certificate
requires two deck crew for the wooden hull's fire watch, which a modern vessel
does not need.

A sailing that cannot be crewed to these numbers is cancelled. It is an operator
cancellation and carries the full disruption entitlements, including a full
refund on a Saver fare. Nobody sails short and nobody may authorise sailing
short, including the Master.

## Hours and rest

- Minimum 10 hours rest in any 24-hour period, of which at least 6 must be
  unbroken.
- Maximum 14 consecutive days on duty, followed by a minimum of 4 days off.
- Maximum 72 hours on duty in any 7-day period.

A crew member who reaches an hours limit mid-roster is stood down, and the
sailing is cancelled if that takes the vessel below minimum crewing. Rest hours
are not a matter of discretion and cannot be waived by agreement between a crew
member and a manager.

## Relief pool

Six crew are held as a relief pool covering sickness, refit periods and leave
across all three routes. The pool has not been resized since MV Lachlan was
withdrawn in March 2024, and the winter timetable reduction is partly what keeps
it sufficient. It is the reason a single unplanned absence in winter can cancel
a sailing rather than being covered.

## Rostering

Rosters are published four weeks in advance. A published roster is changed only
by agreement with the crew member affected, except where a vessel becomes
unavailable, in which case crew are reassigned to the relief roster at the same
hours.

Overtime is voluntary and cannot be used to work around a rest-hours limit.

## Qualifications

Every Master holds a certificate for the class and route. Route knowledge is
route-specific: a Master certified for the Kilmore route is not thereby
certified for the Ashcombe crossing, and vice versa. This is why the relief pool
cannot simply be moved between routes at short notice, and it is the most
common reason a sailing is cancelled while crew are apparently available.

The heritage endorsement for MV Ardmore is held by four Masters in total. If
none is available, the Harbour Circular does not run that day and no substitute
vessel is provided.
