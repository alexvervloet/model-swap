# Disruption and weather

This policy applies when Meridian cancels, delays or curtails a sailing. It
overrides the passenger cancellation policy in every case, including for Saver
fares, which are refundable in full when the company cancels.

## Who decides

The Master of the vessel decides whether that vessel sails, and that decision
cannot be overridden by shore management. The Duty Operations Manager may
suspend a whole route or a whole day's service across the fleet.

A sailing cancelled because minimum safe crewing cannot be met is an operator
cancellation and carries the same entitlements as a weather cancellation. See
the crewing policy for the minimum numbers.

## Weather limits

Sailings are suspended when the forecast or observed conditions at the exposed
part of the crossing exceed these limits.

| Vessel class | Sustained wind | Other limits |
|---|---|---|
| Coastal | Above 45 knots | Significant wave height above 4 metres |
| Estuary | Above 35 knots | Visibility below 200 metres |
| Heritage | Above 25 knots | Sea state above moderate; any forecast thunderstorm |

Gusts are not the measure. A forecast gusting to 50 knots with sustained winds
of 38 does not close the Kilmore route, and it frequently produces complaints
from passengers who have read a weather app rather than this policy.

Visibility below 200 metres closes the Ashcombe crossing regardless of wind,
because the channel is narrow and the crossing is short enough that radar-only
passage saves no time worth the risk.

## What passengers are entitled to

When Meridian cancels a sailing, every passenger holding a ticket for it may
choose one of the following, whatever fare class they hold:

- A full refund, paid to the original payment method within 10 working days.
- A free transfer to any sailing on the same route within 14 days, subject to
  space, with no amendment fee and no fare difference payable.

In addition:

- **Delay of more than 3 hours.** A meal allowance of £15.00 per passenger.
- **No further sailing that day, passenger away from home.** Overnight
  accommodation up to £120.00 per person per night, plus reasonable transfer to
  and from the accommodation, arranged by the terminal office where possible and
  reimbursed on receipts where not.
- **Vehicle stranded.** Secure parking at the terminal is provided free until
  the next sailing the vehicle can be carried on.

These entitlements are not discretionary and do not require a complaint to
obtain. Terminal staff may authorise the meal allowance and the accommodation
directly.

## Curtailed and diverted sailings

A sailing that departs but cannot complete the crossing returns to the port of
departure and is treated as cancelled. A sailing diverted to a different port is
not cancelled, and Meridian arranges onward road transport to the scheduled
destination at its own cost. Passengers who decline the transport and make their
own way are reimbursed to the value of the transport that was offered, not to
the value of what they spent.

## Notification

Cancellations are notified by email and SMS to every affected booking as soon as
the decision is made, and shown on the departure board and the website. Where a
cancellation is decided the previous evening, notification goes out that
evening rather than waiting for the morning.
